Tier 2: Emotional Depth (Strengthens the core experience)
Mood check-in before/after sessions — A quick "How are you feeling?" slider that Solence references in her response and tracks over time.
Reflection journal — Auto-generate a short written summary after each session so users have something to revisit.
Daily themes / gentle prompts — "Today, what felt heaviest?" — a soft daily nudge to encourage engagement without being pushy.
Memory she references — Solence mentions things from past conversations naturally ("Last week you mentioned…"). Builds the "she evolves" promise.
Breathing/grounding mini-experience — A 60-second guided breath using the orb animation, for moments of overwhelm.
Tier 3: Trust, Safety & Privacy (Critical for a wellness app)
Crisis detection — If Solence detects concerning language, gently surface the 988 hotline mid-conversation, not just on the disclaimer.
Face ID / passcode lock — Conversations are deeply personal. Let users lock the app.
Delete conversation history — Easy access in settings, with confirmation.
Export your data — GDPR-friendly download of all conversations.
Tier 4: Engagement & Retention (Use carefully — don't make it gamey)
Optional gentle reminders — "Solence is here when you need her." Not streaks, not pressure — soft.
Weekly reflection summary — A beautifully designed weekly recap of themes, emotions, and growth.
Share a moment — Generate a beautiful quote card from a meaningful Solence reply that users can share (great for organic marketing).
Tier 5: Accessibility & Reach
Adjustable text size — Important for an emotional/calming app used in vulnerable moments.
Light mode polish — Make sure light mode is as beautiful as dark mode.
Localization — Spanish first (huge wellness market), then others.
Tier 6: Marketing & Growth
Referral / share Solence — "Invite someone who needs this." One free week for both.
Apple Sign-In — Faster onboarding, required by Apple if you have other auth.
